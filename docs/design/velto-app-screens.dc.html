Type

Latin: Schibsted Grotesk (closest open match to the current face; keep the current one if licensed). Bangla: Noto Sans Bengali. Load both in one font stack; the browser falls through per glyph.

Home in-progress card: progress bar only, with "current step · step n of 5" under it; the five step names appear only on order detail.

Scale: 28/1.15 screen title (30 on entry screens) · 22/1.2 card title · 17 section · 16 row value · 15 body · 14 secondary · 13 caption · 12 meta · 11 eyebrow, 700, tracking .08em.

Bangla: same sizes, line height 1.65 everywhere, no letter-spacing on eyebrows, Bangla digits for dates, counts and prices; order ids, phone numbers and names stay Latin.

Weights: 700 titles, 600 labels and buttons, 400 body. No italics.

Spacing and shape

4 px grid. Screen gutter 20. Card padding 16 to 20. Card gap 12; section gap 24 to 28. Row height 52 min. Tab bar 64 plus safe area.

Radius: 12 buttons, inputs, banners · 16 cards · 24 sheet top · 999 chips and pills.

Hit targets 44 px minimum, including text links (padding, not margin). One primary button per screen.

Tailwind: these map to rounded-xl, rounded-2xl, p-4/p-5, gap-3, h-13 (add 52 px to the scale).

Behaviour

Home state card: order in progress wins; else last order exists and customer has ordered within 60 days → Same as last time; else over 60 days → Welcome back; else first-time. Default slot: tomorrow, same window as the last order, never today.

Expected return: from the Collected timestamp, +48 h ironing, +72 h wash and dry cleaning, rounded to the next delivery window. Shown as "Usually back by". Hidden once Delivered. Ops override field later.

Booked step: shows "Waiting for our call" until Ops marks the order confirmed; only then "Confirmed". Never claim a call happened.

Payment: no payment numbers, no bKash or other methods in the app. The Payment due box says "Pay the rider at your next pickup or delivery, or message us on WhatsApp to settle it now." and holds one WhatsApp button, prefilled with the order number and the amount due.

Support hours: "A person answers, 10 AM to 10 PM." (Bangla: "একজন মানুষ উত্তর দেয়, সকাল ১০টা থেকে রাত ১০টা।"). Keep it as one config string so Ops can change it. Location copy says "at Velto"; never name an outlet or plant.

Day picker: GET /capacity returns the next 7 days with slotsLeft per window. Render only days with capacity, starting tomorrow; label the first "Tomorrow" when it is. Full windows are omitted.

Feedback: "All good" stores a private flag, shows a thank-you and an optional Google review link. "Something was off" opens WhatsApp prefilled with the order number. Neither is a public rating.

Install: detect platform. Android (beforeinstallprompt available): one Install button. iOS Safari: one line with the Share icon, no button. Both get "Not now".

Preferences: one group, Shirts: No preference (default), On hangers, Folded. Only an explicit pick is sent. No starch, fragrance, "separate whites" or free-text care note; the footer points to WhatsApp for anything about a specific garment. The Profile row subtitle mirrors the pick ("Shirts on hangers") or reads "No preference".

Counting: copy never says the rider counts at the door. Everywhere: "We count and check your items after pickup. The item list and final price then appear here." [Pending Ops: if door count is confirmed as the rule for every pickup, revert to door-count copy; curtains and bulk still go through outlet intake.]

Weekly pickup: never offered on the booking success screen. The "Make this weekly?" card appears on order detail after the customer taps "All good" on a delivered order, or on Home once completedOrders ≥ 2. Also reachable from Profile.

Ready substate: under the Ready step, a pill from dispatch data: "Delivery not planned yet" (no dispatch row), "Delivery today, 4 to 8 PM" (dispatch window set), "Rider is on the way" (dispatch started). Still five steps.

Mixed service: an order can hold items with different services. Service label becomes "A + B"; each item row shows its own service under the name. "Same as last time" rebooks the whole set.

OTP: one real <input> (inputmode="numeric", autocomplete="one-time-code", maxlength 6, paste and WebOTP supported) drawn as six boxes; never six separate inputs.

Book: a summary with rows, each opening a bottom sheet. Unavailable windows are hidden, not shown as "Too late". The first-order offer appears only if orderCount is 0. Submit is never disabled; validation happens inline on tap.

Notifications: a single prompt after the first successful booking, copy "Order, pickup, care and payment updates, and a reminder when your usual pickup is due. Offers only if you turn them on." Turn on enables order, pickup, care, payment and reminders; offers stay off. Profile › Notifications shows the same defaults (reminders on, offers off), one switch per category.

Profile: list of sub-screens; each saves on back with a "Saved" label, no save buttons.

Offline: cache only the app shell and icons; /app/* stays network-only. Screen shows the banner "You're offline. Connect to see your latest orders.", the card "Your orders aren't saved for offline viewing. Connect to load your latest orders, addresses and prices.", a retry, and the WhatsApp link.

Order data: every timestamp, item count and tracker line comes from Ops data; nothing is inferred. A step with no timestamp shows no time. An order with no recorded count shows no count anywhere in its tracker, and the "What we collected" block explains it. Before items are counted, the Due row is hidden and the total reads "Set after counting".

Booking success: shows "Booking reference" with the request reference the API returns. VEL order numbers appear in Orders only once Ops creates the order.

What changes in the current build
  • Home: remove pickup details, routine form, notifications card, "See prices" link. Add state logic and header pills.
  • Book: replace the form with the summary rows plus sheets. Rebuild the item picker as single-service rows with steppers. Delete the summary box and "Signed in as".
  • Prices: becomes a sheet; delete the route from the tab bar's Home highlight bug.
  • Orders: collapse the empty in-progress box to one line; add the in-progress card; list rows show service, date and price only. Missing item counts are explained on order detail, nowhere else.
  • Order detail: eight cards become three blocks; delete rating, history and duplicate title; add computed return date, feedback buttons, delivery pill under Ready, per-item service.
  • Booking success: remove the weekly toggle and the VEL number; show the booking reference. Notification copy updated.
  • Service worker: drop Home and Orders from the cache; shell and icons only.
  • Profile: split into sub-screens with auto-save; remove both save buttons and the empty address form.
  • Order detail, payment due: one WhatsApp button inside the amber box, prefilled with order number and amount due. No payment methods.
  • Order detail, booked: hide the Due row until items are counted.
  • Book: Service row and item picker support mixed service ("A + B", service under each item); rebook repeats the whole set.
  • Notifications: prompt copy and defaults as above; reminders on, offers off.
  • Preferences: shirts only (No preference, On hangers, Folded); remove starch and fragrance.
  • Offline card and support-hours copy replaced as above.
  • Data fixes to request from backend: order count mismatch (4 vs 8) between languages; translated service names; Collected timestamps on every order.
Metrics that prove it works
  • Second order within 30 days, share of first-time customers. The one that matters.
  • Rebook share: bookings made from the Home state card or "Book this again" vs the Book tab.
  • Open to booked: median seconds from app open to the success screen. Target under 30 s for returning customers.
  • Lapsed reactivation: share of 60+ day customers who book within 14 days of opening the app.
  • Tracking views per order and "where is my order" WhatsApp messages per week; the first should rise, the second fall.
  • Weekly pickup adoption and its 8-week survival.
  • Notification opt-in after the first booking, and opt-out rate.
  • Bangla share of sessions and whether its booking completion matches English.
