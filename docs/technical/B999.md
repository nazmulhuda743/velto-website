# B999: 3 Regular Blankets ৳999 (Phase 1)

Status: **BUILT and TESTED** (unit tests, staging SQL, local browser QA). Production needs
`docs/technical/sql/website_b999.sql`.

Route: `/blanket-cleaning-uttara` (English) and `/bn/blanket-cleaning-uttara` (Bangla). Staff: Admin → **Blanket ৳999**
(`/admin/b999`, dispatch section).

## Business rules: one place
`src/lib/campaigns/b999.ts` holds:
- the offer code, package, quantity, ৳999, and the list prices ৳400 / ৳450;
- the ৳50 heavy add-on;
- the **daily cap (4)**, the pickup days (7, from tomorrow, no closed weekdays);
- the sectors (1–18) and the turnaround (3–4 days).

It also holds `b999Total()` / `b999AfterDecline()` (the price maths) and the WhatsApp texts. The page copy is in
`src/content/i18n/b999.ts` (English and Bangla). A unit test rejects em dashes, discount or savings words,
"Wash + Iron", and superlatives.

**Price source:** the live Ops price list, `Blanket (Regular/Medium)` = ৳400 and `Blanket (Large/Heavy)` = ৳450
(Wash & Iron rows, because blankets are washed). It was checked on 8 Oct 2026. The page and the API check it on every
request (`b999PricesOk`). If the prices ever differ, the package is not offered and the page sends people to
WhatsApp.

## Booking (`POST /api/b999` → `website_b999_book`)
- **Browser input:** name, phone, sector, address, pickup date and attribution. Anything else is ignored, including
  price, quantity, service or coupon.
- **Fixed values:** the database fixes offer_code `B999-WEB`, package `B999`, service `Blanket Cleaning`, item_type
  `Regular Blanket`, quantity 3, package_price 999, BDT, `WEBSITE`, `BLANKET` and `Pay at delivery`. These are CHECK
  constraints, so no row can hold anything else.
- **Same pipeline:** the booking becomes the same Ops pickup task and `website_leads` row as every website booking
  (`website_create_request`). Today, dispatch, utm attribution and the per-phone rate limit all work as usual.
- **Fixed task note:** the task note says "Offer: B999-WEB · 3 Regular/Medium blankets · ৳999 fixed · Pay at
  delivery. Do NOT apply the first-order 10% or any coupon." and gives the heavy-blanket rule. Admin → Bookings and
  Today show a **B999** badge and never the 10% badge.
- **The 10% exclusion:** `/api/b999` never calls `offerForBooking()` / `couponForCaller()`, so no 10% or coupon
  line can be written. The 10% logic is unchanged for every other order. Because the task's source is
  `website_booking`, a B999 booking counts as the customer's first website order (the owner decided this on
  8 Oct).
- **Cap and race safety:** a `pg_advisory_xact_lock` is taken per pickup day, then the day's bookings are counted. At 4
  the result is `day_full`. Tested on staging with two bookings fired in the same millisecond (two pg_cron
  workers): one saved, and the other waited about 5 s and got `day_full`.
- **Duplicate submits:** the dedupe key is kept per browser session (sessionStorage). A reload or a second tap
  returns the same booking, and the success screen comes back.
- **Outside Uttara:** no booking is created; the page shows the WhatsApp panel.

## Price confirmation (reclassification)
The Ops order and its total are created by staff in the Velto Ops app; the website cannot lock them. On
Admin → Blanket ৳999:
1. Staff **record a price check**: regular and heavy in the pack, plus extras. The proposed total follows the
   rules, and the booking stays ৳999.
2. Staff call the customer, then **record the answer** (phone, WhatsApp or in person; who and when):
   - Approved: the proposed price.
   - Declined: the declined heavy blankets are returned unprocessed, and the rest is recalculated (3 eligible =
     ৳999, fewer = list price).
3. The Ops order is linked (or found automatically through the dispatch job). A **Mismatch** badge shows when the
   Ops total ≠ the recorded price.
